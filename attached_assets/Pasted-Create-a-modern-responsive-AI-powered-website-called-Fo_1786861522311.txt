Create a modern, responsive AI-powered website called **“Food Health Advisor”**.

### Core Idea

The website allows a user to **scan a food dish using their camera or upload a food image**. The AI identifies the dish, estimates its nutritional characteristics, checks them against the user's health profile, and tells the user:

1. Whether the food is generally suitable for them.
2. Why it may or may not be suitable.
3. Which nutritional factors are concerning.
4. **A healthier alternative specifically chosen for that person's health condition.**
5. Why the alternative is a better choice.

The website should behave like a **personalized nutrition guidance tool**, while clearly stating that it does not replace professional medical advice.

---

## 1. User Health Profile

Before scanning food, allow the user to create a health profile.

Ask for:

* Age
* Gender (optional)
* Height (optional)
* Weight (optional)
* Health conditions
* Allergies
* Dietary restrictions
* Vegetarian/non-vegetarian preference
* Foods they want to avoid
* Foods they prefer

Allow multiple health conditions to be selected.

Examples:

* Diabetes
* High blood pressure
* High cholesterol
* Heart disease
* Weight management
* Kidney-related dietary restrictions
* Food allergies
* Other dietary restrictions

---

## 2. Scan Food

Create a large **“Scan Food”** button.

When clicked, allow the user to:

* Open the device camera.
* Take a picture of the food.
* Upload an existing image.

Show the captured image and provide:

**“Analyze My Food”**

button.

---

## 3. AI Food Recognition

Use AI/computer vision to identify the food.

For example:

**Detected Food: Chicken Biryani**

Show an AI confidence score, such as:

**Food Recognition Confidence: 91%**

If the AI is uncertain, ask the user to confirm the detected food instead of presenting uncertain information as fact.

---

## 4. Nutrition Analysis

Estimate or retrieve nutritional information for the detected food.

Display:

* Calories
* Carbohydrates
* Sugar
* Protein
* Total fat
* Saturated fat
* Sodium
* Fiber
* Estimated portion size

Use attractive nutrition cards and charts.

Clearly label image-based nutritional values as **estimates**, because ingredients and portion sizes can vary.

---

## 5. Personalized Health Analysis

Compare the detected food with the user's health profile.

Display an overall result:

### 🟢 Generally Suitable

The food appears reasonably compatible with the user's profile.

### 🟡 Eat With Caution

The food may be acceptable in a limited portion or with modifications.

### 🔴 Consider an Alternative

The food may be unsuitable for the user's specific dietary needs.

Do not simply classify foods as universally “good” or “bad.” Explain the reason based on the user's profile.

---

# 6. CONDITION-BY-CONDITION ANALYSIS

If the user has multiple health conditions, analyze the food separately for each condition.

Example:

**Detected Food: Chicken Biryani**

| Health Condition  | Result     | Explanation                                                   |
| ----------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Diabetes          | 🟡 Caution | Rice portion may provide a high carbohydrate load             |
| High BP           | 🟡 Caution | Sodium content can vary considerably depending on preparation |
| Weight Management | 🟡 Caution | Large portions can be calorie-dense                           |

---

# 7. ⭐ PERSONALIZED ALTERNATIVE FOOD FEATURE

This is one of the most important features of the website.

After analyzing the food, the AI must recommend **healthier alternative foods specifically suited to the user's health profile**.

Do not just say:

> “Avoid chicken biryani.”

Instead, show:

### Better Alternative For You

**🥚 Boiled Egg + Vegetable Salad**

**Why this may be a better option:**

* Higher protein
* Lower carbohydrate load than a rice-heavy meal
* Can be prepared with little added salt
* Provides vegetables and fiber

Also show:

**Alternative 2:** Grilled chicken + vegetables
**Alternative 3:** Brown rice/chicken vegetable bowl

The alternatives must depend on the person's health condition, dietary restrictions, allergies, and food preferences.

---

# 9. Alternative Comparison

Create a section called:

## “A Better Choice For You”

Show the original food and alternatives side by side.

Example:

**Your Food**
🍛 Chicken Biryani

**Better Options**
🥚 Boiled Egg + Vegetables
🍗 Grilled Chicken + Vegetables
🥗 Chicken Salad

For each alternative show:

* Calories
* Carbohydrates
* Protein
* Fat
* Sodium
* Why it may be better for the user's profile

---

# 10. Portion Recommendation

The system should also provide practical portion guidance where appropriate.

Example:

**Instead of:**
🍛 Large plate of chicken biryani

**Consider:**

* Smaller rice portion
* More vegetables
* Adequate protein
* Limit high-sodium sides

Do not provide rigid medical prescriptions. Use general nutrition guidance and encourage professional advice where appropriate.

---

# 11. Ingredient Warning

If possible, identify likely ingredients from the dish.

For example:

**Chicken Biryani may contain:**

* Rice
* Chicken
* Oil/ghee
* Salt
* Spices
* Fried onions
* Yogurt

If an ingredient cannot be confirmed from the image, clearly mark it as:

**“Possible ingredient — please verify.”**

For users with allergies, prominently display:

### ⚠️ Allergy Warning

“Image analysis cannot reliably confirm every ingredient. Always verify ingredients before eating if you have a serious food allergy.”

---

# 12. Explain WHY

The AI should never only display:

❌ “Don't eat this.”

Instead explain:

**Why?**

**What is the concern?**

**What could you choose instead?**

**Why is the alternative potentially better for your profile?**

This makes the website educational and useful.

---

# 15. Important Medical Safety

The application must NOT claim to diagnose diseases or prescribe treatment.

Include this disclaimer:

> **“Food Health Advisor provides AI-generated general nutrition information and is not a medical diagnostic or treatment tool. Nutritional information obtained from an image is an estimate and may vary based on ingredients, preparation method, and portion size. If you have a medical condition, food allergy, or medically prescribed diet, consult a qualified healthcare professional before making dietary changes.”**

For severe allergies or medically restricted diets, the application should prioritize **ingredient verification and professional guidance** rather than making a definitive recommendation.
